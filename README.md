# OET_ubs-open-license
This is re-sharing our adaptation of [UBS open-licensed resources](https://github.com/ubsicap/ubs-open-license) to the [Open English Translation](https://github.com/Freely-Given-org/OpenEnglishTranslation--OET)

We are very grateful to the United Bible Societies for generously making their *UBS Dictionary of Biblical Hebrew* and *UBS Dictionary of the Greek New Testament* freely available.

Understandably, their notes are based on their own translations, whereas our [Open English Translation](https://OpenEnglishTranslation.Bible) makes a number of different translation decisions (especially concerning people's names),
so we have adapted the UBS dictionary entries to be more relevant to the *OET*.

These adapted resources are then used on our [Open Bible Data](https://github.com/Freely-Given-org/OpenBibleData) site which is accessible [here](https://Freely-Given.org/OBD/par/).
